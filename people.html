Principal Investigator

Lindai Xie, Ph.D. (opens in a new tab)

Assistant Professor of Counselor Education

Department of Educational Psychology, Leadership, & Counseling · College of Education · Texas Tech University

Dr. Xie earned her Ph.D. in Counselor Education and Supervision and her M.Ed. in Counselor Education (College Counseling and Student Affairs) from Virginia Commonwealth University, where she also served as a Graduate Assistant and Adjunct Professor. Her research focuses on college student well-being, especially posttraumatic growth and protective factors among sexual assault survivors and the mental health of international students; ethical AI integration in higher education and counselor education; and research integrity and data quality.
